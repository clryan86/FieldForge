    Worldwide map sources and purchase holds

    Free source · preparation required

    Worldwide OpenStreetMap coverage

    Geofabrik provides free, regularly updated regional OSM source extracts. These are source data, not ready-to-open MBTiles. They require a tile-building pipeline, map-style attribution, and ODbL review before FieldForge can publish packages.

    Browse Geofabrik extracts · OSM tile-server use policy

    Paid · quote and license required

    Worldwide vector streets and places

    MapTiler Planet offers up-to-date global OpenStreetMap-based vector tiles. FieldForge currently opens raster MBTiles only; it cannot render vector/PBF MBTiles. Confirm offline redistribution rights and pack format, and finish vector rendering before purchase.

    Inspect the global dataset · Request the B2C/B2B-capable Custom quote

    Paid · high-resolution quote required

    Worldwide satellite imagery

    MapTiler offers global satellite datasets for offline/on-prem use. Its published medium-resolution imagery uses 2020–2021 source imagery and WebP MBTiles; high-resolution licensing requires a tailored quote. FieldForge must verify the licensed file and offline redistribution terms before publishing it.

    Inspect the global medium-resolution dataset · Ask about high-resolution/offline licensing

    Free · U.S. marine charts

    NOAA nautical MBTiles

    NOAA publishes regional MBTiles derived from its electronic navigational charts and updates baseline packages weekly. This is a U.S. marine-chart source, not worldwide land/street coverage.

    Find NOAA regional MBTiles

    Purchase hold: do not buy MapTiler On-prem Standard for FieldForge distribution. The listed $2,500/year tier is limited to one internal production app and excludes B2C/B2B; the Custom tier is the one that lists consumer/business use and requires a quote. No paid data is included or for sale here.

    Existing regional files: four user-shared OSM packages cover Kansas, Nebraska, North Dakota and South Dakota (about 1.45 GiB total download). They are prepared .ffmap indexes with original source files, not MBTiles; the road graphs are unavailable. These files are not hosted in this portal or yet supported by the current GitHub build.
