Add geographic bounds Optional · for maps with known coordinates

Use a north-up map with a latitude/longitude grid or Web Mercator projection. Enter the outer-edge bounds of the full displayed image in WGS 84 decimal degrees. Borders, cropped legends, rotation or another projection will make these calculations incorrect. Image GPS tags and embedded georeferencing are not read.

Image geographic bounds

West greater than east means the image crosses the date line. For the whole world, use −180 to 180. Web Mercator latitude is limited to ±85.0511287798066°.

No geographic bounds applied.

The bounds file contains your calibration and an image fingerprint, not the image itself. Reopen it with the exact same image. The page forgets images and bounds when closed; saving is always your choice.
