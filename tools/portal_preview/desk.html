Supported files and limits

WGS 84 GeoJSON (RFC 7946), with longitude first and latitude second in the file. Supports Point, MultiPoint, LineString, MultiLineString, Polygon with holes, MultiPolygon and GeometryCollection. Up to 1,000 features, 10,000 positions and 2,000 geometry parts per file. Blank geometries are counted; malformed or unsupported geometry rejects the whole file. Polygon rings must close. Legacy CRS declarations and edges spanning more than 180° longitude are rejected; export date-line crossings as separate parts.

Property text is displayed as text, never as links or code. Feature exports preserve geometry, properties and optional ID; other top-level feature metadata is omitted. No topology, source accuracy or access-rights check is performed. Keep the original file. MBTiles, PBF/vector tiles, shapefiles, KML and GeoTIFF do not open in this viewer.

Take your collection with you

Save for your next session.

Collection JSON saves every place; CSV and GPX use your search results.

Reopen collection JSON here to retain the original numbers and labels. CSV works with the FieldForge GPS place catalog. GPX works with the desktop Places import. Both export coordinates to seven decimal places; that does not establish their accuracy. Exports contain names, precise locations and source notes in plain text.

Import formats and what is kept

Collection JSON must be a FieldForge place collection. CSV needs name,latitude,longitude and optionally source; other columns are rejected. Use UTF-8 and WGS 84 decimal degrees. Imports add records and skip identical name/coordinate/source combinations. An invalid record rejects the whole file.

This collection keeps names, coordinates and source notes. It does not keep GPX tracks, elevation or additional metadata. Keep your original files. Clearing a GPX or image does not remove places already copied into this collection. CSV adds a protective apostrophe to formula-like labels; JSON preserves your original text. GPX writes +180° longitude as the equivalent −180° meridian.
